Albert Einstein (14 March 1879 – 18 April 1955) was a German-born theoretical physicist who is best known for developing the theory of relativity. He also made important contributions to quantum mechanics.[1] His formula E=mc2, which follows from relativity, has been called "the world's most famous equation".[2]

Born in the German Empire, Einstein moved to Switzerland in 1895 & renounced his German citizenship the following year. He received the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics “for his services to theoretical physics”.
