Physics

Scientific study of matter and energy

Physics is the natural science that studies matter, its motion and behavior through space and time, and the related entities of energy and force.

Physics is one of the oldest academic disciplines. Over much of the past two millennia it was a part of natural philosophy, together with chemistry and some branches of mathematics.

History

Early civilizations studied the motions of the stars. Later, Isaac Newton formulated the laws of motion, and Albert Einstein developed relativity.

Classical physics
Physics before about 1900, including mechanics and electromagnetism.
Modern physics
Relativity and quantum mechanics.
